About Cambridge 311 Explorer

This interactive explorer visualizes non-emergency service requests submitted through Cambridge's Commonwealth Connect (SeeClickFix Open311) system.

Data sources & freshness

  • Service requests: Commonwealth Connect Service Requests (2z9k-mv9g) on Cambridge Open Data. The snapshot refreshes nightly via GitHub Actions and automatically fetches newly submitted requests live on page load.
  • Geographic boundaries: Official City of Cambridge GIS layers (cambridgegis_data) for CDD Neighborhoods, 2020 Census Tracts, Election Wards & Precincts, and ZIP Codes.
  • Population figures: 2020 Decennial Census counts from Cambridge Open Data (rqau-pwa4) and Cambridge GIS Election Wards & Precincts.

Tips for exploring

  • Brush the timeline: Drag the left/right edges to resize your date window, drag the middle to scrub through time, or click and drag anywhere on the chart to pick a custom period.
  • Animate over time: Pick a window size (like 1M or 3M), turn on Lock scale in Region Density mode, and press Play (or Spacebar) to watch seasonal patterns move across Cambridge.
  • Cross-filter anywhere: Click any neighborhood/tract on the map or any bar in the charts to filter every view simultaneously.
  • Inspect individual complaints: Switch to Individual complaints and click any dot to read the full resident description, see submitted photos, and open the ticket on SeeClickFix.
